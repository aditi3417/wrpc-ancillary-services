# Assumptions Log

| ID | Assumption | Reason | Status |
|----|------------|--------|--------|
| A01 | Each Monday–Sunday week is assigned to the calendar year containing its Thursday, i.e. at least 4 of its 7 days (ISO 8601 week-year). The same rule is applied to months (our extension; ISO defines week-years only). CY2025 = 30.12.2024–28.12.2025 (52 weeks); CY2026 to Aug = 29.12.2025–30.08.2026 (35 weeks). | Gives every week exactly one year with no overlap; follows a recognised standard. | Confirmed |
| A02 | Two-digit years in titles (dd.mm.yy) are 20yy. | All listed files are from 2023 onwards; no 19yy dates are plausible. | Confirmed |
| A03 | A title date written as "revised dd.mm.yyyy" (without "on") is treated the same as "Revised on" / "Updated on". | 6 titles on the 2026 tab use this form; the meaning is the same. Used only for the date_mismatch check, never for ordering. | Confirmed |
| A04 | Titles naming specific stations (e.g. "Revised TRAS SRAS & SCUC of TPCL_Mundra", "Revised TRAS of GMR Warora") get scope UNKNOWN and are excluded from the step 02 provisional selection. | They do not clearly match a defined scope and look like partial (station-level) revisions; letting them supersede a full statement would drop other stations. They are still on the download list and flagged for manual inspection. | Superseded by A07 (now scope STATION_SPECIFIC, handled as overlays) |
| A05 | Upload timestamps from filenames (ddmmyyyyHHMMSS) are compared as naive local times. | Only their relative order is used, and all come from the same site. | Confirmed |
| A06 | If any candidate for a week x section has no valid upload_ts, no file is selected for it and it is flagged "unranked". | Ordering would otherwise be a guess. (No in-window cases at present.) | Confirmed |
| A07 | Revisions are modelled as base plus overlays: the newest file covering a whole section is the base; narrower revisions (emergency or station-specific) are applied on top. Older overlays are assumed to be incorporated in a newer base, to be verified against the data. | Narrow revisions (one station, or one table) must not replace a full statement for all stations; a base uploaded after an overlay is expected to already include it. As of the current fetch, no older overlays exist: every overlay was uploaded after its week’s base. | Confirmed |
